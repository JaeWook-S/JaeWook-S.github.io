### Welcome to Jaeyuk's page! 👋

Hi! I am studying **AI** at Inha University. 
<br>
My research interests lie in 🧠 Reinforcement Learning, 🤖 Robotics, 🤝 Multi-Robot Motion Planning.

<br>
Driven by my interest in how robots can move efficiently and autonomously in complex real-world environments, I aim to develop learning-based motion and planning methods for robotic systems. I am particularly interested in reinforcement learning for humanoid locomotion and motion generation, as well as multi-robot motion planning and coordination in dynamic environments.


<br>
I have worked on various projects spanning robotics, reinforcement learning, and computer vision. Most notably, I participated in RoboCup 2026 as the team leader, where I worked on vision, behavior planning, collision avoidance, and reinforcement learning-based walking for humanoid robots. I have also developed reinforcement learning-based autonomous driving policies in F1TENTH and conducted research on deep learning-based medical image generation and classification.
